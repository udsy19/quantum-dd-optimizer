Dynamical decoupling of open quantum systems was introduced by \citet{Viola1999} (LIT-01).
\citet{Uhrig2007} proposed optimized $\pi$-pulse sequences (LIT-02), and \citet{Cywinski2008}
analysed how to enhance dephasing times in superconducting qubits (LIT-03).
\citet{Biercuk2009} studied optimized DD in a model quantum memory (LIT-04).
DD has also been used for noise spectroscopy: \citet{Bylander2011} with a superconducting flux
qubit (LIT-05) and \citet{Alvarez2011} for colored noise (LIT-06). Robust DD was studied by
\citet{Souza2011} (LIT-07), and \citet{Ezzell2023} surveyed DD performance on superconducting
qubits (LIT-08). Learning-based approaches include \citet{Rahman2024} (LIT-09),
\citet{Tong2025} (LIT-10) and \citet{Tong2026} (LIT-11).
