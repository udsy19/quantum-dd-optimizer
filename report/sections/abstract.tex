We study model-based, hardware-aware dynamical decoupling (DD) for a superconducting qubit
that sits idle while neighbouring qubits are measured. The planned study combines
filter-function analysis of Gaussian stationary dephasing, a finite-pulse simulator,
synthetic noise spectroscopy with held-out validation, and CMA-ES optimization of pulse
timings and phases, compared with canonical DD sequences at equal pulse number, duration and
constraints. \textbf{Status of this draft:} the software is under construction and all
experiments are \notrun; this report therefore contains no numerical results. No quantum
hardware has been accessed or used (claim PROV-01). Results: \pending{to be generated from
recorded runs}.
